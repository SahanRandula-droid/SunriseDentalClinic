1. Logging in

  1. Open the system in your web browser. The login page appears.
  2. Type your username and password, then click Log In.
  3. Tick Remember my username if you always use this computer. Your password is never remembered.
  4. If you do nothing for 30 minutes you are logged out automatically and must log in again.

Never share your password. Everything you save is recorded under your username.

2. Register a new appointment

  1. On the Main Menu choose Register New Appointment.
  2. Type the patient's name, contact number (10 digits starting with 0, for example 0771234567) and address.
  3. Choose the dentist and the treatment type. The estimated cost appears below the form.
  4. Choose the date (today or later, up to 180 days ahead) and the time (8:00 AM to 7:30 PM).
  5. Click Save Appointment.
  6. The system shows the new appointment number (for example APT-2026-0001). Write it on the patient's card.

A dentist cannot have two patients at the same time. If the time is taken, the system tells you and you choose another time.

3. Display appointment details

  1. On the Main Menu choose Display Appointment Details.
  2. Type the appointment number and click Search (or press Enter).
  3. The patient and appointment information is shown, together with the status: BOOKED, COMPLETED or CANCELLED.

4. Calculate and print a bill

  1. On the Main Menu choose Calculate and Print Bill.
  2. Type the appointment number and click Find Appointment.
  3. Choose a discount if the patient qualifies (senior citizen 10%, child 5%). The total updates straight away.
  4. Check the amounts: Total = treatment cost + consultation fee - discount.
  5. Click Save Bill and Show Receipt and confirm.
  6. Click Print Bill and hand the receipt to the patient.

A bill can be saved only once. To print it again later, search for the same appointment number on this page.

5. Cancel an appointment

  1. Find the appointment (see section 3).
  2. Click Cancel Appointment and confirm.
  3. The status changes to CANCELLED and the time slot becomes free for another patient.

Only a BOOKED appointment can be cancelled. A cancelled appointment cannot be billed or re-opened; register a new one instead.

6. Daily schedule

  1. On the Main Menu choose Daily Schedule. Today's appointments are shown.
  2. To see another day, choose the date and click Show Schedule.
  3. Click an appointment number to open its details, or click Print for a paper copy.

7. Reports (administrators only)

  1. On the Main Menu choose Reports.
  2. Choose the From and To dates and click Show Reports.
  3. Revenue by treatment type shows the income from each treatment.
  4. Dentist workload shows how many appointments each dentist had and how many were cancelled.

8. Exit the system safely

  1. Click Exit System at the top right of any page (or on the Main Menu).
  2. Confirm by clicking Yes, exit.
  3. Wait for the message "You have exited safely", then close the browser window.

Always exit before leaving the desk so that nobody else can use your login.
